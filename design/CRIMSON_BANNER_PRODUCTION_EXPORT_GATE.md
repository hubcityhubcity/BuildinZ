# Crimson Banner production export gate

The [portrait review](CRIMSON_BANNER_EXPORT_REVIEW.md) covered the current fifteen CC-F06 masters. The [collection consistency standard](COLLECTION_CONSISTENCY_STANDARD.md) also requires architectural extremities to sit at least 3% inside each edge. At 1024×1536 that means at least 31 horizontal and 47 vertical pixels. The earlier portrait pass established visibility and name accuracy. It did not measure those edge clearances.

## Crown of Flame III

Compared the selected original and [CC-F06 v2](../assets/editions/crimson-commons-crown-of-flame-stage-3-cc-f06-v2.png) in native-resolution entrance crops. The source has a narrow gold diamond. The v2 candidate has a wider four-point gold diamond within the same burgundy pediment. Retain v2 as the proposed finish master. This delegated ornament decision preserves the pediment, entrance lettering, structural ribs and source. Final export gates still apply.
