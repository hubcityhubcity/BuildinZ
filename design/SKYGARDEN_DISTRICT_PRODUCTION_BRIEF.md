## District language

- **Palette:** emerald, mint, sky blue, pearl and warm wood.
- **Atmosphere:** bright airy dawn after light rain; clean reflections and generous sky separation.
- **Architecture:** branching pearl frames, warm-wood bands, emerald/sky-blue glazing, trellis canopies and physically supported light sky structures.
- **Greenery:** controlled planted ribbons, trellises, sculpted trees and modular planters; never dense, overgrown or name-obscuring.
- **Separation:** avoid Neon Heights cyberpunk saturation, Golden Crest gold-palace massing and Shadow Market noir/obsidian language.
- **Framing:** complete arrival/base, highest roof/crown and all supported bridges must remain visible.
