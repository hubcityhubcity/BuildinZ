# Stage I pixel art direction

William's November 19 direction prioritizes 50 standalone Stage I architectural portraits in refined HD isometric pixel art. The pixel halves of the Neon Residences and Midnight Lounge tests guide rendering. Preserve existing architecture and exact registry names. Leave quiet sky and open street space for later scene layers. Existing cinematic artwork remains historical reference. Pause edition renders, rarity assignment, stages II and III and downstream release work until the base set is complete.

The Stage I archive passed ZIP integrity and all 50 PNG entries decoded. Visual review found 49 isolated dark-background sprites and one contact sheet filed as the fifth Crimson Commons building. Review their near-edge site platforms at native resolution before final selection.
