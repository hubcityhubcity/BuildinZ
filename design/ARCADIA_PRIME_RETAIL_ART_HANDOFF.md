## Production method

Built-in image generation used Arcadia Prime Library retail crops as palette/style references and the activated production brief as the controlling architecture specification. Lyceum Galleria was required to grow beyond the Library composite's near-unchanged massing into a multi-level stocked shopping galleria with visible escalator circulation. Infinite Forum used the selected Lyceum output plus Stage I/III reference evidence and required three stacked retail atria, multiple escalator banks, supported galleries/promenades and the family's largest attached dome.

The native Lyceum output decoded at 1086×1448. It was non-destructively preserved in the temporary production workspace, proportionally resized with point sampling to 1024×1365 and placed at the bottom of a 1024×1536 navy canvas. This normalization adds only sky reserve, preserves the entire architecture and creates the canonical Stage II master below.

Both prompts required refined high-definition isometric pixel art, exact single signage, complete framing, future sky/street reserve and no baked-in people, vehicles, sky events, street incidents or edition traits.
