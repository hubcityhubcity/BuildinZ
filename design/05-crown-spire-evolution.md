# BuildinZ — Crown Spire / Neon Heights Building 005
**Status:** Proposed creative direction; no artwork approved yet.  
**Purpose:** Final landmark identity completing the five-building Neon Heights pilot.  
**Lineage:** Crown Spire → Sovereign Apex → Crown of Neon.

## Art DNA
Crown Spire is the **civic and cultural skyline icon** of Neon Heights, not a residence, mall, nightclub, or technology headquarters. Its design language is ceremonial and monumental: crystalline dark metal/glass, glowing indigo-violet edges, cyan structural highlights, and gold-white light at the crown. Retain the same high-detail, nighttime cyberpunk architectural-rendering style approved for Neon Residences, Pulse Plaza, HoloTech and Midnight Lounge.

**Three non-negotiable rules:** silhouette changes dramatically at every stage; each later form introduces exclusive architectural structures and coherent physical support; three clean, fully framed individual artworks with no panels, infographics or embedded title bars. No excessive trees, no pools at ground level, and no borrowed HoloTech orbital ring or Midnight eclipse canopy.

## Stage I — Crown Spire / The Beacon
- A beautifully detailed, *slender but modest-height* urban landmark with a tall pointed faceted crystal pinnacle, elegant angular buttresses and a small public ceremonial plaza.
- **Distinctive signature:** an illuminated faceted crown-like beacon and narrow vertical light fins framing the center tower.
- Symmetrical monument silhouette, identifiable even as a thumbnail; no giant crown yet. Integrate a visible suspended observation gallery, but leave Stage II's bifurcated apex structure exclusive.
- Feeling: the founding monument of Neon Heights — aspirational and recognizably central.

## Stage II — Sovereign Apex / The Ascendant Crown
- **Significantly taller and architecturally wider** than Stage I, with a split pair of soaring tapered support towers flanking a larger central faceted spire.
- **Exclusive signature:** a large diamond-shaped elevated observation pavilion *suspended structurally between the two tapered support towers*, connected by enclosed glowing skybridges.
- Expanded base/plaza, widened monumental terraces and stronger indigo-to-gold beams; structural scale and height are unmistakable.
- Important: not simply Stage I stretched; the twin flanking pinnacles and huge diamond pavilion change the full identity/silhouette.

## Stage III — Crown of Neon / The City's Throne
- Skyline-defining monumental *multi-spire crown formation* much taller and broader than Stage II. A crystalline central jewel-shaped pinnacle surrounded by 5–7 distinct tapered spires. Luminous gold-violet faceted crown architecture connects the supporting spires in stepped arcs **without creating a HoloTech-style orbital data ring**.
- **Exclusive signature:** a gigantic three-dimensional **crown of sculpted light and glass** anchored directly to the monumental spires, with a luminous suspended central jewel and upper public sky observatory.
- Multiple physically plausible vertical platform zones and ceremonial beams illuminate the city; full jewel and all crown tips must be inside the image frame.
- Feeling: apex landmark representing the entire Neon Heights neighborhood, prestigious and unmistakable even in silhouette.
