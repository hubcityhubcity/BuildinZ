# Shadow Market production brief

Recorded November 4, 2026 (America/Chicago).

## Authority and status

Shadow Market's neighborhood theme comes from the Foundation working set and remains a delegated production decision. All five identity names and stage paths are delegated production decisions recorded in `COLLECTION_REGISTRY.json`; they are not recast as prior owner approvals.

This brief controls base-master production only. A generated image is not selected until its actual output passes visual review and receives a versioned repository path, dimensions, byte length, SHA-256 and Git blob evidence.

## Neighborhood identity

- **Palette:** obsidian, smoked violet, acid green, aged copper and black glass.
- **Style:** noir mercantile district with hidden passages, layered architectural signs and sharp silhouettes.
- **Lighting:** late-night indigo atmosphere, warm copper interiors and restrained acid-green wayfinding. Acid green is an accent, never the dominant facade color.
- **Materials:** matte obsidian stone, smoked-violet glass, patinated copper, blackened steel, dark masonry and sparse wet-stone reflections.
- **Architecture:** isometric/three-quarter 3D presentation; deep overhangs, screened passages, knife-edge rooflines, stacked shadows and physically supported bridges.
- **Separation from Neon Heights:** avoid magenta/cyan cyberpunk saturation, chrome dominance and floating holographic structures.
- **Separation from Golden Crest:** avoid ivory ceremonial symmetry, champagne-gold dominance and sunburst crowns.

## Collection-wide production rules

1. Exact stage-specific identity name appears once as mounted architectural lettering; building type remains separate metadata.
2. Stage II must be materially taller and more capable than Stage I. Stage III must be unmistakably taller, more complex and more important than Stage II.
3. Preserve one recognizable lineage anchor across all three stages without reusing an unchanged building.
4. Keep the complete base, highest roof/crown and all required bridges inside frame.
5. Hidden passages must still be physically plausible and visually readable; no unsupported floating structures.
6. Residential stages use private resident amenities only. No public ground-level pool or retail row.
7. Greenery is sparse and shadow-tolerant; do not soften the noir silhouette into a garden district.
8. Every selected output must be visually inspected. Rejected generations remain uncounted and uncommitted.

- correct building function;

- physically supported bridges, rings and observation structures;

- clear evolution escalation;
- Shadow Market palette and silhouette separation;
- no prohibited public pool, placeholder, watermark or extra label.
