## Canonical foundation

- Neighborhood: **Royal Mirage** (N06)
- Registry provenance: foundation theme adopted under delegated authority
- Palette: sapphire, turquoise, rose gold, sand, mirrored black
- Style: desert-future luxury with shaded courtyards, reflective towers and jewel accents
- Camera/output: premium stylized isometric or three-quarter 3D architecture, portrait 2:3, complete base and crown, exact mounted stage-specific name

Royal Mirage must remain distinct from Golden Crest ceremonial classicism and Neon Heights cyberpunk. Rose gold is structural trim, not full-surface gilding. Greenery is restrained and architectural. Water is elevated/private when used; no ground-level public pool.

1. Exact registry name appears once and no other text appears.
2. Stage II is taller/more complex than Stage I; Stage III is decisively taller/broader than Stage II.
3. Building type remains legible and separate across Residential, Retail, Technology, Entertainment and Landmark.
4. Every bridge, lounge, gallery, ring, screen and terrace is visibly supported.
5. Complete approach/base and highest canopy/crown remain inside frame.
6. Reject hotel ambiguity in Residential, retail ambiguity outside Retail, palace ambiguity outside Entertainment and minaret ambiguity outside Landmark.
7. Never count generated or contact-sheet imagery as final NFT-certified until edition rendering, export validation, permanent URIs and release audit pass.
