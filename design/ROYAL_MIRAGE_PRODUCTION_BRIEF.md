## Canonical neighborhood language

- **Neighborhood:** Royal Mirage (N06)
- **Palette:** sapphire, turquoise, rose gold, sand and mirrored black
- **Style:** desert-future luxury with shaded courtyards, reflective towers and jewel accents
- **Output:** 1024 × 1536 portrait PNG; refined high-definition isometric pixel art; crisp deliberate pixel clusters
- **Atmosphere:** warm desert blue hour after light rain; reflective sandstone paving; dry distant landscape
- **Framing:** complete base/approach and highest canopy/crown; generous sky and street reserve for deferred scene layers
- **Typography:** exact mounted stage name once; no other text, captions, logos or watermark

Royal Mirage must remain distinct from Golden Crest ceremonial classicism and Neon Heights cyberpunk. Rose gold is structural trim, not full-surface gilding. Greenery is restrained and architectural. Water, when required, is elevated and private; no ground-level public pool.
