## Canonical neighborhood language

- **Neighborhood:** Old World Steel (N07)
- **Palette:** iron, weathered copper, furnace orange, coal and industrial blue
- **Style:** retrofuturist heavy industry with exposed rivets, trusses and monumental machinery
- **Output:** 1024 × 1536 portrait PNG; refined high-definition isometric pixel art; crisp deliberate pixel clusters
- **Atmosphere:** overcast industrial dusk after rain; wet cobbled approaches; furnace-orange interiors against blue glazing
- **Framing:** complete foundation/approach and highest chimney, canopy or crown; generous sky and street reserve for deferred scene layers
- **Typography:** exact mounted stage name once; no other text, captions, logos or watermark

Old World Steel must remain distinct from Shadow Market noir and Velocity Row streamline modernism. Use renovated factory heritage, civic openness, red-brown brick, coal-black riveted iron, weathered copper, industrial-blue glass and visible structural support. Scene layers—people, crowds, vehicles, cameos, sky events and street incidents—remain deferred.

## Non-negotiable acceptance gates

1. Exact registry name appears once and no other text appears.
2. Stage II is visibly taller or more complex than its selected Stage I base; Stage III decisively exceeds Stage II.
3. Building type remains unambiguous across Residential, Retail, Technology, Entertainment and Landmark.
4. Every bridge, sky lounge, gallery, observation deck, truss, balcony, ring, terrace, canopy and crown is visibly supported.
5. Complete approach/base and highest chimney/canopy/crown remain inside frame, with future sky and street scene space.
6. No people, crowds, vehicles, cameos, sky events or edition traits are baked into architectural masters.
7. Residential water is elevated and private only; no ground-level public pool. Greenery is restrained.
8. Reject hotel ambiguity in Residential, retail ambiguity outside Retail, public-program ambiguity in Technology, palace/hotel ambiguity in Entertainment and occupied-building ambiguity in Landmark.
9. Validate PNG decoding, 1024×1536 dimensions, byte count, SHA-256 and Git blob after upload.
10. Compare all three stages at equal width before selection.
11. Generated, candidate, contact-sheet or mockup imagery is not final NFT-certified. Edition exports, permanent URIs, metadata and release audit remain separate gates.
