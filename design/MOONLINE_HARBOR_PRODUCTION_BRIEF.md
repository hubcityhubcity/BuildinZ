## Canonical neighborhood language

- **Neighborhood:** Moonline Harbor (N08)
- **Palette:** navy, moon silver, aqua, pearl and deep teal
- **Style:** luminous waterfront architecture on visibly supported elevated piers, with tidal reflections
- **Output:** 1024 × 1536 portrait PNG; refined high-definition isometric pixel art; crisp deliberate pixel clusters
- **Atmosphere:** cool moonlit harbor after rain; warm interior light against aqua/teal glazing; restrained violet in sky only
- **Framing:** complete pier supports, access approach and highest mast/canopy/crown; generous sky and street reserve for deferred scene layers
- **Typography:** exact mounted stage name once; no other text, captions, logos or watermark

Moonline Harbor must remain distinct from Neon Heights cyberpunk saturation, Old World Steel heavy industry and Royal Mirage desert luxury. Use pearl shells and curved canopies, navy structural fins, silver trim, aqua/deep-teal glass, substantial piles and an urban waterfront approach. Buildings remain buildings rather than ships. People, crowds, vehicles, cameos, sky events, street incidents, edition finishes and rarity traits remain deferred.
