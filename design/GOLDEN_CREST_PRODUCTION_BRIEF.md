# Golden Crest production brief

Recorded October 31, 2026 (America/Chicago). Golden Crest is a Foundation working theme adopted as a delegated production decision; it is not represented as earlier owner-approved canon.

## District lock

- Palette: champagne gold, ivory, amber glass, royal blue, black marble.
- Mood: sunlit ceremonial luxury, confident rather than gaudy.
- Camera: elevated isometric/three-quarter architectural view, portrait master, complete silhouette and ground plane.
- Materials: black marble bases, ivory stone, warm reflective glass, restrained champagne-metal trim.
- Environment: distinct Golden Crest skyline at late-afternoon-to-blue-hour; no Neon Heights purple city reuse.
- Typography: exact canonical name physically mounted on architecture in readable geometric uppercase. Building type and stage label never replace the name.
- Evolution: each stage must visibly increase height, structural complexity, exclusive amenities and crown importance. Stage III must not be merely a larger Stage II.
- Framing: full crown and base inside frame, clear sky margin, no border, caption, infographic, floating label or watermark.
- Landscaping: restrained formal planting; architecture remains dominant.
- Output rule: create base stage masters before rarity/edition treatments. No placeholder counts as art.
