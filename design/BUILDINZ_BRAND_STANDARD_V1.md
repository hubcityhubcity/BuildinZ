# BuildinZ — Visual Identity & NFT Art Release Standard v1.0
**Status:** Brand direction adopted October 24, 2026. Artwork-specific changes still require visual approval.  
**Applies to:** Neon Heights now; all ten planned neighborhoods before production.  
**Source of truth:** original approved stage concepts, this canon registry and the site's implemented name labels. No existing approved image is silently overwritten.

## Brand promise
One premium architectural NFT universe, multiple distinctive neighborhoods. Every collectible reads as a real piece of futuristic architecture, not a generic AI image or a reskinned tower. Each evolution is a visibly greater architectural achievement, not merely increased height, lighting or recoloring.

## Required visual grammar
1. **Architectural identity:** polished, premium isometric/three-quarter architectural composition; coherent scale and perspective; believable structures, clear primary building, richly rendered details and neon night city context where appropriate.
2. **Palette continuity:** black/navy or charcoal luxury surfaces, electric indigo/violet, magenta and cyan accents; neighborhoods and building categories may use distinct secondary colors. Text must stay legible at mobile thumbnail scale.
3. **True evolution:** Stage II changes building footprint/height/silhouette and has a distinct exclusive feature; Stage III develops a dramatically new silhouette and a new pinnacle signature architecture. Neither is a copy with an added LED strip.
4. **Stage-accurate *building-mounted* name:** the **exact canonical stage name** must appear visibly on the actual structure, mounted on facade, marquee, building-screen or structural illuminated lettering — **not** solely as a floating text overlay, detached label, caption or interface card. Every stage has its own stage-name sign. Stage I may preserve a founding name in secondary signage in later stages, but the evolved name is dominant.
5. **Sign design:** shared futuristic geometric techno-luxury type family (architectural uppercase sans or equivalent), precise letterforms, bright but controlled neon edge illumination, contrast sufficient to read at phone size. Name signs scale with architecture: podium/front on I; integrated mid/high-rise tower or skybridge on II; skyline-readable facade or architectural feature on III. Avoid classical serif hotel type, mismatched handwriting, random letter stylization or generated misspellings.
6. **BuildinZ mark:** a small secondary brand emblem or Z motif may appear consistently, but **never replaces or overwhelms the actual stage name**. The emblem must not be mistaken for the property's name.
7. **Composition:** the *entire* building and signature crown/spire canopy fit inside each final individual image, with safe top margin, centered and `object-fit:contain` behavior. No clipped crowns, cut-off spires, giant blank over-padding, unrelated sidebars, annotations, text-callout boxes, excessive landscaping, or ground-level accessible pools.
8. **No style regression:** never publish abstract wireframe/schematic art as final NFT artwork. Preserve approved architecture, rooftop features and restrained landscaping when correcting signage.
9. **Master/archive:** retain original approved full-resolution image, clean individual stage assets, and any corrections as separate versioned files. Check naming, dimensions, SHA hash, full silhouettes, desktop/Android portrait+landscape clarity, no distorted upscale, and browser rendering before replacing public images.
10. **Review gates:** art proposed → owner approval of specific stage visuals → exact asset upload verified → page/preview and code checks → mobile/desktop QA → merge. Do not equate an approved triptych or mock test with site release. This is a prototype; no mint/wallet/staking benefits are real.
