## District language

- **Palette:** racing red, cobalt, white, gunmetal and amber.
- **Atmosphere:** crisp blue-hour dawn after light rain; reflective dark paving, clean skyline separation and amber track lights.
- **Architecture:** streamlined white exoshells, red motion ribbons, cobalt glazing, gunmetal support/service structure and physically tied-back cantilevered fins.
- **Motion language:** lane-like paving, banked curves, turbine/ring crowns and horizontal acceleration lines; buildings must not become literal vehicles or racetrack props.
- **Greenery:** minimal wind-shaped grasses and small planters; never a lush district or name-obscuring planting.
- **Separation:** avoid Neon Heights cyberpunk saturation, Golden Crest gilded palace massing, Shadow Market noir obsidian and Skygarden pearl/wood garden language.
- **Framing:** complete arrival/base, highest roof/crown and every supported bridge, lounge, gallery or observation structure remain visible.
